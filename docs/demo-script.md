# Gemini Orchard Operations Agent: Demo Workflow

**Theme**: Coordination, not just Chat.

1. **Regional Monitor**: Start at the **Regional Operations Dashboard**. Show the aggregator monitoring the Michoacán Avocado Belt ($1.2M total exposure).
2. **Prioritization**: Use the AI Advisor to "find highest risk municipality." The globe flies to Tancítaro.
3. **Mission Planning**: Click **Plan Drone Mission**. A cyan route appears on the Cesium globe. Explain that Gemini is reasoning about the flight path based on satellite stress.
4. **Multimodal Analysis**: Click **Analyze Inspection Results**. View the detected anomalies (Persea Mite) and Gemini's vision-driven reasoning.
5. **Strategic ROI**: Click **Simulate Intervention ROI**. Show the cost-benefit ($12k vs $145k protect) and the **14-Day Delay Risk**. This is where the business value is proven.
6. **Human-in-the-loop**: Click **Draft Field Task**. Show the drafted SMS/Email for the operator. Note that the agent does not send without human approval.
7. **Operational Memory**: Scroll down to **Recent Ops History**. Explain that **MongoDB MCP** stores these outcomes so the next mission knows if the intervention worked.

**Core Message**: The agent coordinates high-stakes operational work under human oversight to protect regional agricultural yields.
